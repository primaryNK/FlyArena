# FlyArena derivative-work and commercial-use permission

FlyArena welcomes experiments, research ideas, mods, ports, educational uses,
and derivative projects.

FlyArena is source-available under FASL-1.0. Public distribution of a modified
or derivative version, and any commercial use, requires prior written permission
from the FlyArena Licensor unless explicitly permitted below.

## Contributions to the official FlyArena repository

You do **not** need separate derivative-work permission to fork FlyArena,
modify it for the purpose of preparing a contribution, and submit a Pull Request
to the official `primaryNK/FlyArena` repository.

This contribution exception is limited to work intended for submission to the
official FlyArena repository. It does not grant permission to publish or maintain
an independently distributed modified version, mod, port, product, or derivative
project.

Contributors must follow `CONTRIBUTING.md` and accept
`CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md` through the project's CLA process before a
contribution can be accepted.

## Requesting permission for other derivative works or commercial use

For public distribution of an independent modified or derivative version, or
for commercial use, open the **Derivative / commercial permission** issue
template and provide:

1. your name or organization and a reliable contact method;
2. a plain-language description of the proposed work;
3. which FlyArena code, art, formats, or names you plan to use;
4. whether source code and binaries will be public;
5. where and how the work will be distributed;
6. whether it will be commercial, monetized, sponsored, or paid;
7. the intended license for your additions;
8. how BANC and other third-party material will be obtained, attributed, and
   kept under its original terms; and
9. the requested permission duration and territory, if relevant.

Do not include confidential business information in a public issue. Ask for a
private contact channel first if necessary.

Opening an issue, Discussion, Pull Request, or receiving an acknowledgement does
not by itself grant permission.

Additional permission is granted only by a written response from the FlyArena
Licensor that clearly identifies the approved project and the scope of the
permission.
